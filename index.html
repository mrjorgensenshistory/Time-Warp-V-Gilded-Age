TIME WARP V

BUILDING THE RAILROAD

The Gilded Age built the modern world — and someone paid for it.
Step into the shoes of the men who made the money, and the people who paid the price.
The right choice is always what REALLY happened.

The Gilded Age glittered on the outside and rotted underneath — that’s what “gilded” means: gold on the surface, cheap metal below.

A handful of men built empires of steel, oil, railroads, and money, and became the richest people who had ever lived. Others built it with their hands, or were pushed aside by it.

You will live as these real people. Read each scene. Make the choices they faced. Choose what really happened, and history remembers you. Choose wrong, and it’s GAME OVER — back to the start.
